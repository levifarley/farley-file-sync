Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

Farley File Sync is a personal tool used only by its owner, Levi Farley, to access his own Google Drive account.

What data the app accesses

With your permission, the app accesses files and folders in your Google Drive through the Google Drive API, only to carry out the actions you ask it to perform.

How data is used and stored

Data from Google Drive is processed on your own computer. The app does not send your Drive data to any server run by the developer, and it does not sell, rent or share your data with anyone. Sign-in tokens are stored only on your own device.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Farley File Sync's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

You can remove the app's access at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
